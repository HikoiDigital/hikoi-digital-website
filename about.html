About Hīkoi Digital

Hīkoi Digital builds small-business websites along the Queensland coast. We help you put the essentials in one place so customers can see what you do and how to get in touch.

In te reo Māori, hīkoi (HEE-koy) means “to walk”. It’s the idea behind our name: we walk alongside you, from planning your website to handing it over.

Walk the digital road with someone who knows the way.
