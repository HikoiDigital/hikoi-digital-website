Photos, galleries and extra costs

A simple gallery can use photos you supply. We’ll agree on the gallery size and any extra image work before you accept the quote. We’ll also explain domain and hosting costs and quote separately for work like detailed case studies or extra research.

How revision rounds work

Send one combined list of changes within the agreed scope for each revision round.

Help after handover

For 14 days after handover, you can email us with questions about accessing or using your site. We aim to respond within two business days and tell you when to expect an answer.

If something we delivered needs correcting, get in touch. For new work, such as adding a page or updating services, we’ll agree on a price before starting.
