Frequently asked questions

Tap a question to see the answer. If your question is not here, ask us in the enquiry form — we will reply by email.

How much does a website cost?

Simple Start is A$450 fixed build fee for a one-page website. Multi-Page is A$900 fixed build fee for a 3–5 page website. We confirm the fixed build fee and any separate costs upfront, before you decide whether to go ahead.

What is included?

Both packages include a mobile-friendly layout and a website handover.

Simple Start includes: 1 page; website copy drafted from your answers; an enquiry form or a link to your existing booking service; one revision round; website handover.

Multi-Page includes: 3–5 pages; help organising your information and drafting copy for the agreed pages; an enquiry form or a link to your existing booking service; two revision rounds; website handover.

What is not included?

Domain and hosting costs are separate. We will explain those costs and quote separately for work like detailed case studies or extra research before you accept the quote.

Do I need a domain and hosting already?

No. If you already have them, we will use what you have. If you do not, we will explain the domain and hosting costs before work starts.

What do I need to supply?

Fill in the short form on the Get Started page and tell us about your business and what you would like customers to find or do on your website. We turn your answers into website copy. A simple gallery can use photos you supply. We will agree on the gallery size and any extra image work before you accept the quote.

How long will it take?

We will confirm timing when we agree the scope and price with you, before work starts.

I already have a website. Can you update it?

Yes. Tell us about your current website in the enquiry form, and what you would like changed. If you are unsure which package fits, we can talk it through.

How do revision rounds work?

Send one combined list of changes within the agreed scope for each revision round. Simple Start includes one revision round. Multi-Page includes two revision rounds.

What if I need extra work?

For new work, such as adding a page or updating services, or work outside the agreed scope, we will agree on a price before starting.

What happens after handover?

We will show you how to log in and explain how to access your site and the accounts that go with it. For 14 days after handover, you can email us with questions about accessing or using your site. We aim to respond within two business days and tell you when to expect an answer. If something we delivered needs correcting, get in touch.

How do I get started?

Fill in the form on the Get Started page, or email hello@hikoidigital.com. We will reply by email. If we need more detail to recommend a package, we will ask a few questions. You will have the scope and price before you decide whether to go ahead.

Where do you work?

Hīkoi Digital builds websites for small businesses along the Queensland coast, from the Gold Coast to Bundaberg, including Hervey Bay and Brisbane.
